03 / ABOUTResearcher. Educator. Builder.

I am a Ph.D. candidate and Graduate Research Assistant in the School of Computing and Informatics at the University of Louisiana at Lafayette, advised by Dr. Beenish M. Chaudhry.

I develop reproducible pipelines for review analysis, topic modeling, qualitative validation, and multimodal bias auditing. My research interests include natural language processing, human–computer interaction, responsible AI, and deep learning.

Before joining UL Lafayette, I taught computer science at universities in Bangladesh and worked as a Senior Research Engineer at AIMS Lab. My software engineering background helps me connect research questions with working systems.
